## 📊 ASO STRATEGY NOTES (Viktor's Principles Applied)

### ✅ Keywords Strategy (Low-Competition Focus)
| Keyword | Competition | Strategy |
|---------|-------------|----------|
| "choose adventure" | Low-Medium | Primary target in subtitle + keywords |
| "visual novel" | Medium | High intent, included in keywords |
| "text adventure" | Low | Classic genre term, low competition |
| "interactive story" | Medium | Core descriptor, in title flow |
| "branching narrative" | Low | Specific, high-intent long-tail |
| "offline game" | Medium | Key differentiator, user pain point |
| "story game" | High | Must-have despite competition |

### ✅ Metadata Optimization
- **Title:** ChronoCanvas (brand name, distinctive)
- **Subtitle:** "Choose Your Adventure Story" (keywords + value prop)
- **Keywords field:** Long-tail, specific terms rather than generic
- **Description:** Natural keyword integration, benefit-driven structure

### ✅ Conversion Optimization (Viktor's Tips)
1. **Lead with value:** "YOUR CHOICES. YOUR STORY. YOUR ENDING." (emotional hook)
2. **Social proof via depth:** Emphasize variety of stories and multiple endings
3. **Pain point addressed:** "Completely offline" (no WiFi barrier removed)
4. **Genre clarity:** Lists all genres so users know what to expect
5. **Comparison targeting:** "Perfect for fans of..." captures competitor traffic
6. **Content depth:** "All adventures unlocked" emphasizes full experience available

### ✅ Retention Signals in Copy
- "Replay to unlock unique conclusions" (replayability)
- "No two playthroughs are ever the same" (retention hook)
- Multiple story lengths mentioned (15-42 min) for different sessions
